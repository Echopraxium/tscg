QR Code to Pocket City

A QR code is transformed into a navigable 3D miniature city. Dark modules become buildings, light modules become streets — under one binding constraint.

Seen from directly above, the city is still a scannable QR code. Switch to Bird's eye and point a phone at the screen. It should decode the text you typed.

The fertile constraint

A dark module must stay predominantly dark from above; a light module predominantly light. Everything else is free. That single rule is what makes the generation disciplined rather than arbitrary — and it is directly falsifiable, which is rare for a TSCG instance.

Consequences

  • • Orthographic projection is mandatory — under perspective, off-centre buildings lean outward and their roofs stop covering their footprint
  • • Roof caps span the full grid pitch — bodies are inset by 0.002 to avoid z-fighting; the caps close the gap
  • • Chimney caps match the roof exactly — from above the cap is all you see of a chimney
  • • Signs, doors and windows are vertical — invisible from above, therefore unconstrained

Determinism

The same text always produces the same city. The seed is the city's identity card. Successive SHA-256 application walks an ordered sequence of distinct interpretations of one unchanged QR plan.

Stylesheets

A stylesheet is not a colour theme. Its rules block drives geometry — heights, arch budget, fenestration density — so switching one re-interprets the same seed into a different city. Layout is a style channel.

V is exceptionally high because the model carries its own empirical test: point a scanner at the Bird's-eye view and it either decodes or it does not. F is lowest — flow exists (the scanner's reading path, the explorer's traversal) but is not the system's centre of gravity.

Interactive Controls

Auto walk

  • • Routes from the bottom-right corner to the top-left, turning at every wall
  • • The street network inside a QR is not connected — dozens of isolated pockets. What makes a route possible is the quiet zone, the margin the QR spec requires, which rings the whole code
  • • Ring cells cost more to traverse, so the walk crosses the city instead of skirting it
  • • Any movement key hands control back to you

Views

  • • Isometric 1 — orthographic, oblique. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom
  • • Bird's eye 2 — snaps straight down. Tilt freely from there; the selector switches to Isometric on its own, and back when you return to top-down
  • • Walk 3 — street level, eye height locked
  • • Fly 4 — free altitude

Free movement (Walk / Fly)

  • • Click the canvas to capture the mouse, Esc to release
  • • WASD or arrows to move
  • • Shift to run
  • • Space / C for altitude (Fly only)

Zoom

  • • The wheel zooms in every view
  • • Isometric and Bird's eye scale the orthographic frustum — an orthographic camera ignores distance, so moving it would do nothing
  • • Walk and Fly narrow the field of view: the observer stays put

Generation

  • • Build city — encode the text and regenerate
  • • Grid — QR versions only: 21, 25, 29, 33, 37, 41, 45, 49. There is no 32 or 48
  • • Invert I — swap polarity. Built fabric becomes a labyrinth; QR decodability is deliberately lost
  • • Next seed N — SHA-256 of the current seed: same plan, new city

Verifying the claim

  • • Type a distinctive string, press Build
  • • Switch to Bird's eye
  • • Scan the screen with any phone — it should decode your text
