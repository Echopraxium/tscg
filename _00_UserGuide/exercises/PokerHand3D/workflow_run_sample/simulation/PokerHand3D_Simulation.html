PokerHand3D

The 52-card deck laid on a circular pillar: the four vertical edges are the four suits, the fourteen floors are the ranks with the Ace doubled — the bottom floor reserved for the wheel (A-2-3-4-5), the top floor for every other use. Each card is a point (rank × suit). The ten hand categories read as geometric figures.

Reading the figures

  • Pair / straight — bicolour cylinders (a pair = one segment; a straight = a snake)
  • Three / four of a kind — a magenta surface (triangle / square) with bicolour edges
  • Flush — stacked translucent disks of one suit on a single edge
  • Determining card — an octahedron (magenta on-figure, beige on a kicker)

Because the Ace is doubled rather than identified, the illegal round-the-corner straight K-A-2-3-4 is made visually impossible.

About this poclet

A purely structural / informational poclet: it visualises the combinatorial grammar of 5-card hands, not the game of poker (no betting, no opponents). The strength gauge is inversely proportional to a hand's frequency, so rarity explains the ranking.

Source

Standard 5-card poker hand rankings and their exact frequencies over C(52,5) = 2,598,960 hands.
