The lesson: Territory → Map

The disc rotates continuously (the Territory). Your eye — or a strobe — samples it at rate f_s. What you see (the Map) is a reconstruction from those samples, and it can diverge from reality.

Find it yourself

  • In Strobe mode, hit ▶ Speed Scan and watch the readout: Apparent figures climbs 1 → 2 → 3 … up to 12 as ω crosses each ratio p/q.
  • 1 figure = frozen/reversed; q figures = q crisp superimposed yin-yangs ("rayons"), with well-separated grey levels (steps of 1/q).
  • Between locked ratios the copies spread out → a uniform grey blur.
  • Lower f_s → wider spacing of the locks; higher → a denser ladder.
  • Retinal persistence mode instead smears the motion into a continuous blur.

Order-2 symmetry: the figure repeats every half-turn, so freeze and reversal recur twice as often as for a single mark.

Why Attractor is weak

Nothing converges. The "frozen" figure is a fixed point of the perception, not of the disc — an honest, low-A poclet.
