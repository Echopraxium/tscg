## 1. The Hypothesis

The four ontologies of the Layer Cake (M3, M2, M1, M0) are **not a model of
reality**. Three of them — M3, M2, M1 — are a model of the **toolkit's own
architecture**: their job is to keep the four layers *coherent* and *articulated*
with one another. Only **M0** reaches toward a real system — and it does so as a
*scale model* (a poclet / instance), never as a claim about the world.

In one line: **M3–M2–M1 describe the kit; M0 describes a system with the kit.**

## 2. Map, Territory — applied to the kit itself

Core Hypothesis 1 states that *the map is not the territory*: a representation is
never the thing it represents. This hypothesis applies that same distinction
**reflexively, to TSCG itself**.

- The **territory** of an M0 instance is a real system (a fire, a market, a
  cell). M0 is a *map* of that territory — a deliberately reduced one.
- But M3, M2 and M1 have **no external territory at all**. Their "territory" is
  the kit. They are the *map of the map*: the rules by which maps are built and
  made to fit together. They answer "how is a piece made, and how does it snap
  onto the next?" — never "what is the world like?"

This is why internal coherence at M3–M1 is a statement about **construction
discipline**, not about nature. A perfectly coherent Layer Cake proves that the
kit is well-built; it proves nothing about the universe.

## 3. Layer by layer

**M3 — the abstraction layer.** The three monoidal grammars and their primitive
types:
- **Gt** (Territory / Eagle Eye), operator `×` — the ASFID types
  (Attractor, Structure, Flow, Information, Dynamics): what a system *is*.
- **Gm** (Map / Sphinx Eye), operator `+` — the REVOI types
  (Representability, Evolvability, Verifiability, Observability,
  Interoperability): how a system is *known*.
- **Gs** (Stereopsis), operator `|` — the TKSL types
  (Temporality, Knowledge, Symbol, Localizability) plus the polarity poles: the
  depth born of the two eyes together.

M3 is pure architecture: an alphabet of primitives and the laws by which they
combine. It asserts nothing about any particular system.

**M2 — generic concepts, built by composition of the M3 types.** Each generic
concept is assembled from M3 primitives via the monoidal operators
(`×`, `+`, `|`, and the duality `^op`). Combinations of *several* generic
concepts are produced by a naming function:

> **Fm2** : `GenericConcept²⁺ → GenericConceptCombo`

`Fm2` is a **function, not a functor** — the emergence it captures is
*non-compositional* (its arguments are *combined*, and shared dimensions
contract via lattice join; the result is irreducible to any parent). M2 holds
only patterns intended to recur across *several* fields — the "systemic
Esperanto" vocabulary — not the laws of any one field.

**M1 — business domains and the Domain Concept Combos.** M1 adapts the generic
vocabulary to a field (biology, electronics, music, …) and introduces the hybrid
combos that bind a domain to generic concepts:

> **Fm1m2** : `Domain⁺, GenericConcept⁺ → DomainConceptCombo`

Like `Fm2`, `Fm1m2` is a producing **function**. It is where the kit's shared
pieces meet a discipline's specifics — still architecture (how a domain plugs
in), not yet a claim about any real instance.

**M0 — the instance: the scale model of the system at hand.** M0 is the only
layer that touches a territory. It is a *maquette* — minimal yet complete
(a poclet exhibits all five ASFID dimensions) — a proof of concept of a
principle, not an industrial or research-grade model of the real thing.

## 4. What this rules out

Two natural misreadings, both dissolved by the hypothesis:

1. **"M2 contains the true, universal concepts of all systems."** No. M2
   contains the kit's *construction pieces*, coherent *by construction*. Their
   recurrence across fields is a **revisable proposal** (Core Hypothesis 2), not
   a proven law of nature.
2. **"A coherent Layer Cake validates the framework's universality."** No —
   that is precisely the *Icarus* failure mode (over-claiming from internal
   consistency). Self-consistency of the architecture and empirical universality
   are different claims; only the first is established by the layers.

The hypothesis is therefore a **guard-rail**: it keeps the impressive internal
coherence of M3–M1 from being mistaken for evidence about the world.

## 5. Why it matters

- It fixes the **mental model** a KitCrafter needs before touching the layers:
  you are learning an *architecture*, not memorising *facts about reality*.
- It explains why the layers can be judged by **coherence and articulation**
  (does M2 compose cleanly from M3? does M1 plug into M2 via `Fm1m2`?) while M0
  is judged by **fit to a real system** — two different standards, one per side
  of the map/territory line.
- It keeps TSCG honest: the same reflexive move that lets *TSCG model itself*
  (see *Credibility Accretion*, § *TSCG Models Itself*) is bounded here, so that
  self-modelling is never confused with self-proof.

## 6. Relation to the other Core Hypotheses

- **CH1 (Map–Territory)** supplies the distinction; CH4 turns it on the kit.
- **CH2 (Systemic Esperanto)** is what M2/M1 *are* — a proposed shared language;
  CH4 says that language is architecture, not natural law.
- **CH3 (LEGO Technic)** gives the build metaphor; CH4 says the instructions for
  the bricks (M3–M1) are not a diagram of the world, only of the set.
- **Ontological Overfitting** is the discipline that keeps M2/M1 from bloating
  with bespoke pieces; CH4 is the discipline that keeps the whole cake from being
  read as a theory of everything.
