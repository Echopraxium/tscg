A reading order

  1. RDF and XSD: everything is a triple; literals carry datatypes.
  2. RDFS: classes, subclasses, labels. Enough for most small models.
  3. SKOS and DCTerms: name concepts and describe documents.
  4. OWL: when you need richer logic and a reasoner.
  5. SHACL: check that real data has the shape you expect.
  6. PROV-O, DCAT, ADMS, FOAF, schema.org: pick up as your data needs them.

Two worlds to keep apart

OWL reasons in an open world. A missing fact is unknown, not false, so OWL infers new facts rather than reporting errors. SHACL validates in a closed world. A missing required property is a violation. Use OWL to say what things mean and SHACL to check what a file contains.

The name “layer cake” for the Semantic Web stack comes from Tim Berners-Lee's diagrams of the early 2000s. TSCG's own Layer Cake is a separate idea that shares the image.
